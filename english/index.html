How the process works

  1. First contact, free of charge

    You describe your matter by phone, by email or at the office. You learn how the procedure works and what it is likely to cost.

  2. The lawyer reviews your documents

    Decisions, contracts and letters are reviewed. You receive an assessment of your position and a proposal for the next steps.

  3. Your lawyer at the immigration office and in court

    The firm handles correspondence with authorities, opponents and insurers and represents you in court.

Immigration and asylum law

Dr. Ertunc holds the German title of certified specialist in migration law. The firm advises and represents you on matters such as:

  • Residence

    Residence permits, settlement permits, extensions and permanent residence.

  • Naturalisation

    Application, requirements and appeals against refusal.

  • Family reunification and visas

    Bringing a spouse and children to Germany, the visa procedure.

  • Asylum

    Interview, decision and appeal before the administrative court.

  • Expulsion and deportation

    Urgent legal protection and representation before the immigration office.

Connections

A criminal conviction, a divorce or a dismissal can affect your right of residence. Because the firm handles both sides, these effects are checked from the start.

  • § 54 AufenthGGrounds for expulsion after a conviction
  • § 31 AufenthGA spouse’s own right of residence after separation
  • § 18 ff. AufenthGResidence for the purpose of employment
